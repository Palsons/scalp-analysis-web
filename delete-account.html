Delete your Scalp Analysis Pro account

For medical representative accounts

You can ask us to delete your Scalp Analysis Pro account from this page. You do not need the app installed to send a request.

How to request deletion

  1. Enter the name, email address, and employee ID on the account.
  2. Confirm that you want the account deleted.
  3. Submit the form. It opens an email to developer@palsonsderma.com with your details.
  4. Send that email. We will verify the request and delete the account within 30 days.

If the form does not open your email app, send the same details yourself to developer@palsonsderma.com.

Data we delete

Within 30 days of a verified request we delete:

  • Your name, email, phone number, and employee ID
  • Your password hash and sign-in access
  • Your profile photo
  • Your assignment to camps

Data we may keep

We may keep the following for up to 3 years, or longer if the law requires it:

  • Camp visit records (doctor, clinic, city, and dates) needed for the organization’s operations. Your name is removed from those records where it is practical to do so.
  • Patient registrations, scalp photographs, and reports. These are camp records and are not deleted only because a representative account is closed.
  • A short record that a deletion request was received and completed, without your password or profile photo.

To ask about patient images or reports, email the same address and include the patient name and camp, if you know them.
