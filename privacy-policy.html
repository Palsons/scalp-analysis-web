Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 September 2026

This policy describes how Scalp Analysis Pro (“the app”) handles information. The app is used by medical representatives during scalp analysis camps.

Information we collect

When an organization creates a medical representative account, we store:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and employee ID
  • A password, stored only as a secure hash
  • An optional profile photo

When the app is used at a camp, we store:

  • Camp and visit details, including doctor name, clinic or hospital, city, state, and PIN code
  • Patient details entered in the app: name, mobile number, gender, age, and hair concern
  • Scalp photographs captured with the device camera, and the analysis report generated from those images

The app requests camera access only to capture scalp images. It does not collect precise location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.

How we use information

We use this information to sign representatives in, run camp visits, store scalp scans, and produce reports for the organization that operates the camp. We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

Account and camp data is available to administrators of the organization that issued the account. Hosting and storage providers process data only to operate the service. We may disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep account and camp records while the account is active and the organization needs them for operations. If you request account deletion, we delete the account profile as described on the delete account page. Patient scans and reports may be kept as the organization’s camp records where the law or a legitimate operational need requires it.

Security

Passwords are hashed. The app stores the sign-in token in secure device storage. Access to the service is limited to authenticated representatives and administrators.

Children

The app is for medical representatives in a professional setting. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly create accounts for children.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your medical representative account from the delete account page. Patients who want their scalp images or report removed should contact the camp organizer or email us, and we will handle the request with the organization that collected the data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page.

Contact

Email developer@palsonsderma.com for privacy questions.
